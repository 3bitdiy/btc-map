The Project

A living tradition, reconnected

From the 1950s on, socialist Yugoslavia ran a state programme supporting cultural institutions, most visible in its rural residency programmes, the so-called “art colonies.” After the country's break-up, some endured: partly as independent organisations, partly on modest public budgets.

Beyond the Cities builds on that tradition. It works to revitalise existing art colonies and kindred initiatives in Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and North Macedonia, creating a network of independent, resilient cultural partners that strengthen rural areas, and opening new room for transnational collaboration.

Objectives

What the project sets out to do

  • 01

    Build a network

    Through mapping, needs analysis and collaborative capacity-building across the three countries.

  • 02

    Strengthen cooperation

    A shared digital platform, practical “how-to” guides and long-term coordination between organisations.

  • 03

    Partner locally

    Cooperation agreements with regional Goethe-Instituts and a joint steering board.

  • 04

    Sustain the field

    Long-term structural support for rural cultural organisations, lasting beyond the project itself.

Partners
