The 45th symposium brought six sculptors to Kikinda in July: from Serbia, Slovenia, Austria and, for the first time, Georgia. They worked side by side in the big hall of the former brick factory, where the clay comes straight from the pit next door.

## The pieces

Most works this year were large: three of them over two metres, fired in the big kiln over four days. They will stay in the Terra collection and go on show in the sculpture park next spring.

## Next year

The call for Terra 2027 is already out on the notice board. If you run a colony and know a sculptor who should apply, send them our way.
