For two days the studios in the old school building in Sićevo are open to visitors. The eight artists of this year's colony will be there to show what they made in September and to talk about their work.

## When and where

Saturday and Sunday, 28 and 29 November, from 11 to 18 h. The school is at the entrance to the village, a ten-minute walk from the bus stop. Parking is in front of the church.

## What you will see

Paintings and prints made during the colony, the first proofs from our new etching press and a small selection from the collection, which now counts more than four hundred works.

Entrance is free. Children are welcome, and on Sunday afternoon there is a short printing workshop for them.
