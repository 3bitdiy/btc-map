This August brought six sculptors to Jasenovo: from Serbia, Slovenia, Austria and, for the first time, Georgia. They worked side by side in the open-air workshop by the old school, with stone and wood from the hills around the village.

## The pieces

Most works this year were large: three of them over two metres. They will stay in the village sculpture park and go on show there next spring.

## Next year

The call for Jasenovo 2027 is already out on the notice board. If you run a colony and know a sculptor who should apply, send them our way.
