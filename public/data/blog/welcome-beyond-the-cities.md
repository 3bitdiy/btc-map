After months of fieldwork and data-gathering, the **Beyond the Cities** map is
online. It gathers the rural artist colonies of Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia and Serbia into one living picture of the field.

## Why a map

From the 1950s on, socialist Yugoslavia ran a state programme supporting rural
residency programmes, the so-called _art colonies_. After the country's
break-up, some endured. Many have never been mapped in one place, until now.

The map lets you:

- Browse every colony by country, art field and scope
- Open any colony for its location, contacts and activities
- Follow the network as it grows over 2026–2028

## What comes next

This journal will carry news from the colonies, calls for residencies, and
stories from organisers across the region. If you run a colony and want to be
listed or share news, [get in touch](/#project).
