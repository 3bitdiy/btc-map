Galičnik has thirty permanent residents, one road in and no shop. Every August it also has twelve artists, two cooks and a very busy mule. This is how the colony works.

## Getting there

Materials come up in July, before the artists. Canvas, paper and clay travel by van to the last bend and by hand from there. Finished works go down the same way in September.

## Living and working

The artists stay in three restored stone houses. Studios are wherever there is light: the school hall, the church yard, the terrace of the old inn. Dinner is together, every night, and most of the collaborations between colonies in this region started at that table.

## Why it matters

The village lives from the colony as much as the colony lives from the village. The houses are kept in repair, the inn opens, and for one month the children who come to visit their grandparents have something to do.
