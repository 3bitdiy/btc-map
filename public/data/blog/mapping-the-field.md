Building the map meant tracking down colonies that often exist only in local
memory, a summer painting gathering, a sculpture symposium, a village that
hosts artists every August.

## A moving target

Colonies open, pause and return. Some run on modest public budgets, others
purely on the energy of a few organisers. That means the map is never
_finished_, it is a snapshot that we keep correcting.

If you spot something out of date, that is expected. Tell us, and we will fix
it. The data is open and community-driven by design.

## Three countries, one network

The goal is not just a directory but a **network**: shared knowledge, practical
guides, and long-term cooperation between organisations that have often worked
in isolation. The map is where that starts.
